    <h3>FatigueFormer: A Physiology-Informed Model for Upper-Limb Muscle Fatigue Assessment</h3>
    <p class="publication-authors"><strong>Tong Zhang</strong>, Hong Guo, Shuangzhou Yan, Dongkai Weng, Jian Wang, and Hongxin Zhang</p>
    <p class="publication-summary">A physiology-informed model that groups surface electromyography features by fatigue-related trends to assess upper-limb muscle fatigue across individuals and contraction intensities.</p>
    <details class="paper-abstract">
        <summary>Research overview</summary>
        <p>The model separates sEMG measurements according to how they change with fatigue, then learns from relationships within each time window and across windows. Experiments use perceived exertion as a fatigue label, compare generalization to unseen participants, and include a second dataset for external validation.</p>
    </details>
